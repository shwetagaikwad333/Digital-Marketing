
# Digital Marketing Analytics | Marketing Mix Modeling

## Project Overview

This project explores digital marketing campaign performance using data analysis and visualization in Python. It focuses on understanding how marketing spend, campaign activity, and channel performance relate to revenue and other key performance indicators (KPIs).
The analysis uses a dataset containing over 132,000 records to explore campaign performance across marketing channels, business verticals, territories, and customer segments.The goal is to transform raw marketing data into actionable insights that can support data-driven marketing decisions, budget allocation, and performance monitoring.

## Project Objectives

The objective of this project was to evaluate digital marketing performance across paid advertising channels, understand customer acquisition efficiency, and investigate interactions between marketing channels to identify actionable insights.

### 1. Marketing Channel Performance Analysis
- Analyze Google advertising performance across Paid Search, Shopping, Performance Max, Display, and Video campaigns.
- Evaluate Meta advertising performance across Facebook, Instagram, and other placements.
- Assess TikTok campaign performance using relevant marketing metrics.
- Compare cost per click (CPC), cost per impression, and cost per conversion across channels.
- Evaluate channel efficiency across different territories and business verticals.

### 2. Customer Acquisition Analysis
- Examine the relationship between first purchases and total purchases using correlation analysis.
- Calculate customer acquisition cost (CAC) by marketing channel to compare acquisition efficiency.

### Project Outcome

The analysis was designed to support data-driven marketing decisions by comparing channel efficiency, evaluating customer acquisition costs, and exploring how paid and organic channels may interact. The findings can help inform marketing budget allocation and identify areas for further investigation.

## Project Workflow

### 1. Data Understanding and Preparation
- Examined the dataset structure, variables, and data quality.
- Identified missing values and duplicate records.
- Cleaned and transformed relevant fields.
- Standardized currency values, including conversion to GBP where applicable.
- Prepared the data for exploratory analysis.

### 2. Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA)
- Examined revenue, marketing spend, and campaign performance.
- Compared metrics across channels, territories, verticals, and customer segments.
- Investigated patterns and relationships between marketing investment and business outcomes.
- Used visualizations to identify trends, differences, and areas for further investigation.

### 3. Marketing Performance Analysis
- Evaluated CTR, CPC, CAC, revenue, and advertising spend.
- Compared campaign performance across marketing channels.
- Explored correlations between marketing variables.
- Investigated potential halo effects and cannibalization between channels.

### 4. Data Visualization
Developed visualizations using Matplotlib, Seaborn, and Plotly to present patterns in campaign performance and make the analysis easier to interpret.

## Key Areas of Analysis

- **Channel Performance:** Compare marketing activity and performance across Google, Meta, and TikTok.
- **Marketing Efficiency:** Examine spend and cost-related KPIs alongside revenue.
- **Segment Analysis:** Explore performance differences across territories, verticals, and customer segments.
- **Marketing Relationships:** Investigate correlations and potential interactions between channels.
- **Business Insights:** Identify patterns that could inform campaign evaluation and future marketing decisions.

*Note: Correlation and exploratory analysis alone do not establish causal marketing impact or prove incremental return on advertising spend.*
